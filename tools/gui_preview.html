The panels render from the firmware draw code at the ST7796S geometry: the main menu, the SWEEP SETTINGS screen, and the hidden hardware SETUP list, each with the arm animation and the live status bar. Colours are quantised to RGB565, so what you see is what the panel shows.

The palette is chosen for the yellow non-actinic safelight the cleaner runs under. There the eye is adapted to ~580 nm and blue/cyan collapse toward black, so nothing encodes meaning in blue and every pair that must be told apart differs in luminance as well as hue. Amber marks headings, plum marks the changeable half of a settings row, red is the arm and alarms, olive is ON/park.

Editing a value shows the effect immediately — wafer circle size (sized to the 150 mm max and cropped top/bottom), sweep extent (Edge<>Ctr vs Edge<>Edge), the park tick and the red arm arrow. The arm speed follows the cycle time and speed profile; the generator runs only over the wafer, where a pulsing ⚡ appears next to the arrow. Spray on/off starts the mist at the arrow tip and the TIMER in the status bar; Debug mode switches the title between Status Debug and Status Work and the sensor rows between DIS and live values. Drag Setup row to scroll the 20-row list through its 7-row window. To record a GIF, capture a panel (Windows Win+Alt+R or ScreenToGif). The About screen is omitted.
